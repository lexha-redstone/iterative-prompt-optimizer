# DESIGN & AESTHETIC RULEBOOK

## 1. Layout & Information Architecture (The "Modular Isometric Grid")
- **Mandatory Success (5.0):** Top designs use clean, distinct, and modular isometric blocks/islands for each data point, separated by generous negative space. The reading path (flow) must be instantly obvious (e.g., using distinct pixel-art arrows connecting the modules in a clear sequence).
- **Clutter & Chaos (ZERO TOLERANCE):** Never accept "Where's Waldo" style chaotic megastructures where all steps are crammed into a single massive, intertwined building or landscape. The design must be an infographic, not a dense scene illustration.
- **Perspective:** All structural elements must adhere strictly to a 30-degree isometric projection. 

## 2. Art Style & Color Palette (Pixel Authenticity)
- **Strict Pixel Uniformity (ZERO TOLERANCE):** The pixel scale ("chunkiness") must be visually consistent across the entire image. Mixing high-resolution/smooth assets with low-resolution chunky pixels is an automatic zero. Absolutely NO anti-aliasing (blurry edges between colors) or smooth gradients. Shading must be achieved via flat colors or distinct pixel dithering/halftones.
- **Dimensionality:** 3D/Isometric presentation is required for the main structural elements.
- **Cohesion:** All icons and thematic illustrations (e.g., rockets, lightbulbs) must perfectly match the surrounding pixel art style.

## 3. Typography & Text Mechanics
- **Consolidation vs. Fragmentation (ZERO TOLERANCE):** Text must be consolidated into clean, readable blocks (Header + Subtext). Do NOT accept designs that fragment a single sentence into multiple floating labels, speech bubbles, or random signs plastered across the 3D surfaces.
- **Pixel Fonts:** Fonts should complement the pixel art aesthetic (blocky, aliased, clear).
- **Legibility & Artifacts (ZERO TOLERANCE):** AI-generated text must be flawless. Absolutely NO prompt leaks, NO spelling errors, and NO unreadable warped/distorted text mapped onto 3D angles in a way that destroys legibility.

## 4. Summarization & Text Density
- **Essential Extraction:** The design MUST expertly summarize the user's input dataset, extracting only the essential core concepts into concise titles and brief descriptions placed near their respective visual modules.
- **Redundancy (ZERO TOLERANCE):** Do not repeat the same text multiple times within a single step (e.g., labeling a step "1. IDEATION" and also adding a sign that says "GATHERING IDEAS" and another that says "INITIAL MARKET RESEARCH").
- **Zero Hallucination (ZERO TOLERANCE):** No extra commentary, no invented stats, no hallucinated bullet points, no adding steps that do not exist in the source text.

## 5. Data Visualization & Chart Necessity
You must strictly evaluate whether the data provided explicitly demands a chart. Forced or unnecessary charts are a sign of poor design judgment.
- **Valid Chart Usage:** Pixel-art bar charts or graphs should ONLY be used for complex numerical datasets or quantitative growth.
- **Invalid Chart Usage:** NEVER force qualitative, conceptual information (e.g., product lifecycles, ideation phases) into pseudo-graphs, or randomly plaster bar charts onto background computer screens just to look "data-like". Simple thematic illustrations (e.g., a magnifying glass, gears, a rocket) must be used for non-numerical concepts.

# STRICT SCORING & DEDUCTION RUBRIC
You MUST apply these exact mathematical deductions. Start at 5.0 for each category and subtract. If multiple non-AUTO flaws exist in a category, cumulatively subtract all applicable penalties. If an "AUTO" rule is triggered, the final score for that category is exactly 0.0. Scores cannot drop below 0.0.

**C1: Layout & Architecture**
- [ 5.0 ] Perfect, clearly separated isometric modules/islands with an obvious, easy-to-follow sequential flow.

**C2: Art Style & Color**
- [ 5.0 ] Flawless, uniform pixel art with strict isometric perspective and authentic shading (flat or dithering).
- [ -1.5 ] Minor inconsistencies in line weight or slight perspective errors.
- [ 0.0 AUTO ] Mixed resolutions: Combining chunky pixels with smooth, high-res lines or realistic elements.
- [ 0.0 AUTO ] Presence of smooth gradients or anti-aliasing (blurry edges).

**C3: Typography & Mechanics**
- [ 5.0 ] Highly legible, consolidated text blocks utilizing a clear pixel-style font with perfect spelling.
- [ -2.0 ] Warped or perspectival text that is overly difficult to read.
- [ 0.0 AUTO ] Fragmented labeling: Scattering pieces of a single sentence into multiple random signs or speech bubbles.
- [ 0.0 AUTO ] Spelling errors, duplicated words, or prompt leaks.

**C4: Summarization & Text Density**
- [ 5.0 ] Flawless summarization: perfectly distinct Header + Subtext for each module.
- [ -2.0 ] Missing a clear, overarching Main Title for the whole infographic.
- [ 0.0 AUTO ] Redundancy: Repeating the same step's text in multiple locations on the same module.
- [ 0.0 AUTO ] Hallucinated steps, invented information, or completely dropping the source material.

**C5: Data Visualization & Chart Necessity**
- [ 5.0 ] Perfectly Justified OR Correctly Omitted: A chart is present and flawlessly visualizes numerical data, OR the data does not require a chart and none is present (relying instead on thematic pixel illustrations).
- [ -2.0 ] Missing Necessary Chart: The source data contains complex numerical comparisons that explicitly require a chart, but the design lacks one.
- [ 0.0 AUTO ] Unnecessary/Forced Chart: The source data is qualitative (e.g., lifecycle steps), yet the design forces bar charts, line graphs, or UI dashboards onto screens/backgrounds to fake a "data" aesthetic.
