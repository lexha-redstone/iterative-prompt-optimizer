# DESIGN & AESTHETIC RULEBOOK

## 1. Isometric Projection & Structural Geometry (C1)
- **Mandatory Success:** Top designs use clean, distinct isometric blocks/islands. All structural elements must adhere strictly to a 30-degree isometric projection.
- **Zero Tolerance for Flat Elements:** Isometric infographics must maintain unified spatial depth. You must ruthlessly penalize flat, front-facing, or orthographic elements, banners, or major focal points. 

## 2. Pixel Art Authenticity (C2)
- **Strict Uniformity & Aliasing:** Pixel scale must be 100% uniform. No "mixels" (massive chunky pixels next to microscopic pixels). Shading must be achieved via aliased dithering patterns or solid fills. 
- **Zero Tolerance for Fake Pixel Art:** Never accept standard 3D renders masquerading as pixel art. Halftone dots, screentones, smooth gradients, and anti-aliased (blurry) edges on illustrations are instant failures.

## 3. Typography & Text Mechanics (C3)
- **Consolidation:** Text must be consolidated into clean, readable blocks (Header + Subtext). Do not accept fragmented labeling where a single sentence is scattered across random signs.
- **Legibility Rules:** AI-generated text must have perfect spelling. While pixel fonts are preferred, smooth vector typography in dense body text is acceptable for readability purposes, though it incurs a minor penalty compared to perfect pixel text.

## 4. Information Architecture & Data Integration (C4)
- **Modularity vs. Megastructures:** The design MUST expertly compartmentalize the user's source data into distinct visual modules. Never accept a "Megastructure" where separate concepts are merged into a single chaotic, intertwined landscape.
- **Voxel/Spatial Integration:** Conceptual icons, diagrams, or charts must be modeled as physical voxel props inhabiting the 3D space, not floating flat on a 2D plane in negative space.
- **Strict Data Logic:** Never force quantitative charts (bar/line graphs) onto strictly qualitative concepts. 

## 5. Lighting, Palette & Aesthetic Execution (C5)
- **3-Tone Lighting Mandate:** Authentic isometric pixel art relies on distinct, flat planes of light: Highlight, Midtone, and Shadow. You must penalize any curved gradients, soft lighting, or smooth cylindrical shading on what should be blocky forms.
- **Shading Cohesion:** The shading technique must be strictly uniform. Mixing retro dithering with modern color banding is an immediate structural failure.
