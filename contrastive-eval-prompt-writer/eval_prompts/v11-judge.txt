# DESIGN & AESTHETIC RULEBOOK

## SCORING HIERARCHY (CAPS, ANCHORS, DEDUCTIONS)
1. **Auto-Fails (0.0)** overrule everything.
2. **Max Caps** define the absolute maximum ceiling for a score.
3. **Min Anchors** define a protected baseline. If a core foundational requirement is met, the score CANNOT drop below this anchor, protecting it from death-by-thousand-cuts deductions.
4. **Targeted Deductions** are subtracted cumulatively from the starting score (5.0) or Max Cap, but they stop at the Min Anchor if one applies.

## 1. Isometric Projection & Structural Geometry (C1)
- **Foundational Integrity Anchor:** If the core macro-structures (e.g., platforms, architectural blocks) maintain strict 30-degree isometric projection, their foundational score is protected.
- **2D UI Obscuration:** Flat title banners receive a localized deduction, but if massive front-facing 2D text boxes, ribbons, or unwarped overlays obscure >30% of the canvas, the spatial depth is ruined, resulting in a total Auto-Fail.
- **Impossible Geometry:** Blending incompatible viewpoints (e.g., top-down perspective mixed into an isometric environment) is a fundamental spatial breakdown.

## 2. Pixel Art Authenticity (C2)
- **Mixel Auto-Fail:** True pixel art uses a singular pixel grid. Automatically fail any image utilizing mixed pixel resolutions or varying pixel grid scales across different assets, typography, or shading (e.g., massive pixel blocks for titles but tiny pixels for body text).
- **Halftone Exemption:** Aliased 1-bit halftones or dense screentones are valid and exempt from the "Fake Pixel Medium" auto-fail, provided they strictly adhere to and accurately shade valid isometric planes.

## 3. Typography & Text Mechanics (C3)
- **Cascading Spatial Break:** Flawless spelling/pixel fonts yield 0.0 points if the foundational isometric structure (C1) is broken, or if the text is exclusively utilized within flat 2D overlays that destroy spatial immersion.
- **Typographic Housing:** Text must map to the Z-axis space or serve as unobtrusive HUD elements. 

## 4. Information Architecture & Data Integration (C4)
- **Modular Isolation vs. Visual Clutter:** Data points must be highly scannable, separated by distinct 3D negative space. Chaotic, overlapping structures, redundant floating labels, or spaghetti flow lines destroy infographic utility.
- **Flow Destruction:** Flat 2D elements (giant arrows) that aggressively cross over the Z-axis confuse the spatial hierarchy and trigger severe deductions.

## 5. Lighting, Palette & Aesthetic Execution (C5)
- **Authentic Shading:** 1-bit checkerboarding or valid dithering accurately mapped to 3D planes guarantees high lighting scores.
- **Geometric Prerequisite:** Lighting cannot be evaluated on flat planes. If the core focal piece is 2D, isometric lighting inherently fails.
