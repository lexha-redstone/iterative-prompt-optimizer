# DESIGN & AESTHETIC RULEBOOK

## 1. Isometric Projection & Structural Geometry (C1)
- **Mandatory Success:** Top designs use clean, distinct isometric blocks/islands. All structural elements must adhere strictly to a 30-degree isometric projection.
- **Strict Spatial Geometry:** Isometric infographics must maintain unified spatial depth. You must ruthlessly penalize flat, front-facing, or orthographic elements integrated into the 3D space.
- **HUD Exemption:** Flat 2D elements serving strictly as UI/HUD overlays (e.g., main title banners anchored to the top of the canvas) are EXEMPT from 'Mixed Perspectives' penalties, provided they do not interact physically with or cast shadows onto the 3D isometric floor.

## 2. Pixel Art Authenticity (C2)
- **Strict Uniformity & Aliasing:** Pixel scale must be 100% uniform. No "mixels" (massive chunky pixels next to microscopic pixels). Shading must be achieved via aliased dithering patterns or solid fills. 
- **Zero Tolerance for Fake Pixel Art:** Never accept standard 3D renders or illustrations masquerading as pixel art via cheap filters. Halftone dots, screentones, smooth gradients, and anti-aliased (blurry) edges are instant failures.

## 3. Typography & Text Mechanics (C3)
- **Consolidation:** Text must be consolidated into clean, readable blocks. Do not accept fragmented labeling where a single sentence is scattered across random signs.
- **Pixel Fonts Only:** Text MUST conform to the pixel aesthetic. Standard smooth vector graphics, anti-aliased text, or high-res fonts glued over pixel art completely breaks the aesthetic illusion and must be zeroed out.

## 4. Information Architecture & Data Integration (C4)
- **Modularity vs. Megastructures:** The design MUST expertly compartmentalize the user's source data into distinct visual modules. Never accept a "Megastructure" where separate concepts are merged into a chaotic, intertwined landscape.
- **Voxel/Spatial Integration:** Conceptual icons, diagrams, or charts must be modeled as physical voxel props inhabiting the 3D space. Standard 2D infographics masquerading as isometric art must be severely punished.
- **Strict Data Logic:** Never force quantitative charts (bar/line graphs) onto strictly qualitative concepts. 

## 5. Lighting, Palette & Aesthetic Execution (C5)
- **3-Tone Lighting Mandate:** Authentic isometric pixel art relies on distinct, flat planes of light: Highlight on top, Midtone on one side, Shadow on the other. 
- **Global Cohesion:** UI drop-shadows or faux-ambient occlusion behind floating text boxes fundamentally contradict the physical, global lighting rules of a unified isometric environment.
