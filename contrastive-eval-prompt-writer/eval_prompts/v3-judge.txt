# DESIGN & AESTHETIC RULEBOOK

## 1. Isometric Projection & Structural Geometry (C1)
- **Mandatory Success:** Top designs use clean, distinct isometric blocks/islands. All foundational structures must adhere strictly to a 30-degree isometric projection.
- **Catastrophic Spatial Collapse:** You must ruthlessly penalize designs that abandon the isometric grid. If flat, front-facing, or top-down orthographic flowcharts take up >30% of the canvas, the spatial illusion is broken.
- **HUD & Minor Label Exemption:** Flat UI elements (e.g., floating stage names, top banners) superimposed over a successful, strictly isometric environment only incur a minor penalty, provided they do not cast conflicting shadows on the 3D floor. 

## 2. Pixel Art Authenticity (C2)
- **Strict Uniformity & Aliasing:** Pixel scale must be 100% uniform. No "mixels" (massive chunky pixels next to microscopic pixels). Shading must use true aliased dithering or solid fills. 
- **Zero Tolerance for Fake Pixel Art:** Never accept standard 3D renders or digital line-art masquerading as pixel art via cheap filters. Halftone noise filters, screentones, smooth gradients, and anti-aliased (blurry) edges are instant failures.

## 3. Typography & Text Mechanics (C3)
- **MAX CAP Logic:** "MAX CAP" limits dictate the *highest possible score*, not the baseline floor. If typographic chaos warrants a 0.0, the score remains 0.0, not the cap limit.
- **Hallucinations & Spam:** AI spelling errors, illegible scribbles, or copy-pasting the same text label repeatedly as spatial filler completely destroy the infographic's utility. 

## 4. Information Architecture & Data Integration (C4)
- **Modularity Anchors:** The overarching layout is paramount. Successfully compartmentalizing sequential/categorical data into cohesive 3D islands or stepped topographies must be heavily rewarded before penalizing minor aesthetic prop flaws.
- **Spaghetti Architecture & Horror Vacui:** Interconnecting modules with chaotic 2D arrows, overlapping non-isometric flowcharts, or stuffing negative space with irrelevant data charts are fatal layout failures. 

## 5. Lighting, Palette & Aesthetic Execution (C5)
- **3-Tone Lighting Mandate:** Authentic isometric pixel art relies on distinct, flat planes of light: Highlight on top, Midtone on one side, Shadow on the other.
