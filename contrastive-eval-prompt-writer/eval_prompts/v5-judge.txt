# DESIGN & AESTHETIC RULEBOOK

## 1. Isometric Projection & Structural Geometry (C1)
- **Mandatory Success:** Top designs use clean, distinct isometric blocks/islands. All foundational structures must adhere strictly to a 30-degree isometric projection.
- **HUD Exemption & Minor Props:** Flat 2D title banners framing the top/edges (HUD) do not violate spatial integrity and incur no penalty. Minor flat props (e.g., flat clocks, UI icons) placed within a 3D space result in scalable deductions, not instant failures.
- **Catastrophic Spatial Collapse:** You must ruthlessly penalize designs that completely abandon the isometric grid for >50% of the canvas or create architectural impossibilities (Escher geometry). 
- **Perspective Contradiction:** Mixing projections—such as placing a flat or front-facing orthographic centerpiece alongside isometric peripheral elements—mandates immediate spatial collapse.

## 2. Pixel Art Authenticity (C2)
- **Strict Uniformity & Aliasing:** Pixel scale must be 100% uniform. No "mixels" (massive chunky pixels next to microscopic pixels). 
- **Dithering vs. Noise:** Stylized, face-aligned 1-bit dithering (Macintosh style) is permitted. However, unaligned, global halftone/noise filters, screentones, and anti-aliased (blurry) edges used to masquerade standard 3D renders/line-art as pixel art are instant failures.

## 3. Typography & Text Mechanics (C3)
- **Typographical Severity Scale:** AI hallucinations in primary headers trigger an AUTO 0.0. A single hallucination in secondary descriptive text results in a scalable deduction. 
- **MAX CAP Logic:** "MAX CAP" limits dictate the *highest possible score*, not the baseline floor. If typographic chaos warrants a 0.0, the score remains 0.0, not the cap limit.

## 4. Information Architecture & Data Integration (C4)
- **Modular Islands Anchor:** Flawlessly separating distinct data points onto non-touching, self-contained isometric platforms mandates top scores. 
- **Physical Data Flow:** Progression between isometric nodes must be represented physically (stairs, ramps, isometric segmented arrows). Flat 2D flow lines incur heavy caps.
- **Spaghetti Architecture & Horror Vacui:** Unprompted chaotic macro-structures, non-linear tangled loops, and duplicated redundant labeling are fatal layout failures. 

## 5. Lighting, Palette & Aesthetic Execution (C5)
- **3-Tone Lighting Mandate:** Authentic isometric pixel art relies on distinct, flat planes of light: Highlight on top, Midtone on one side, Shadow on the other.
