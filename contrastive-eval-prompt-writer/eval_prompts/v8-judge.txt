# DESIGN & AESTHETIC RULEBOOK

## 1. Isometric Projection & Structural Geometry (C1)
- **Macro-Structure Supremacy:** Top designs use clean, distinct isometric blocks/islands. All foundational structures must adhere strictly to a 30-degree isometric projection. If the foundational environment is strictly isometric, localized minor flat props should not ruin the score.
- **Strict HUD & Signage Exemption:** Standard infographic UI elements (floating speech bubbles, text banners, flat UI icons) AND camera-facing signboards/projection screens ("billboarding") are strictly ALLOWED with no penalty, provided they act as visual overlays or signage and the underlying base architecture adheres to the 30-degree grid.
- **Embedded Flat Geometry Penalty:** Physical objects meant to exist *within* the 3D scene (e.g., a clipboard or laptop sitting on a desk) that are drawn completely orthographically (flat/front-facing) trigger severe deductions.

## 2. Pixel Art Authenticity (C2)
- **Isolate Typography:** Do NOT evaluate the rendering style of text/fonts in this category. C2 evaluates the artwork and environment ONLY. 
- **Strict Uniformity & Aliasing:** Pixel scale must be uniform. "Mixels" (mismatched pixel grid sizes in the art) are a major flaw. 
- **Noise vs. Pixel:** Unaligned, global halftone/noise filters, screentones over vector art, and soft anti-aliased edges used to masquerade standard smooth digital art as pixel art are instant failures.

## 3. Typography & Text Mechanics (C3)
- **Decoupled Accuracy Focus:** Evaluate C3 strictly on typographical accuracy, perfect spelling, and legibility. Flawless spelling/transcription must be rewarded even if the pixel art medium (C2) fails, provided the layout (C4) and perspective (C1) succeed.
- **Medium Aesthetic Standard:** If the typography is completely anti-aliased and utilizes standard modern vector fonts (e.g., Helvetica, Arial) rather than pixel fonts, the score is capped at 3.0, regardless of perfect spelling, to reflect a failure to integrate text into the pixel medium.

## 4. Information Architecture & Data Integration (C4)
- **Modular Islands Anchor:** The layout MUST utilize distinct, non-intersecting isometric islands or platforms separated by clear negative space. Translating abstract 2D concepts (like funnels or flowcharts) into physically elevated 3D platforms is the highest standard of this medium.
- **Spaghetti Architecture & Structural Chaos:** Environments where 3D platforms unnecessarily overlap, lack breathing room, or utilize illogical physical connectors that confuse the reading path must be penalized.

## 5. Lighting, Palette & Aesthetic Execution (C5)
- **Lighting Mandates:** Standard palettes require 3 distinct, flat planes of light (Highlight, Midtone, Shadow). 1-Bit/Monochromatic palettes use varying densities of pixel dithering to simulate these faces. 
- **1-Bit Constraint Exemption:** In 1-bit/monochromatic pixel art, applying dither-density shading to environmental planes while leaving small character sprites flat is a highly authentic constraint-based stylistic choice and incurs no penalty.
